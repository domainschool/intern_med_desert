Medication "Desert" Locator


Non-profit medical networks, regional hospital outreach systems, and state health departments lose millions of dollars annually due to the inefficient allocation of mobile clinics, pop-up pharmacies, and vaccine drives.

Under the Affordable Care Act (ACA), hospitals face heavy financial penalties for high 30-day readmission rates. When vulnerable patients (elderly, low-income, non-drivers) live in isolated zones, they skip critical medications. This leads to preventable health crises that flood Emergency Departments, resulting in uncompensated emergency care costs.

By spatial-mapping these medication deserts, planners can accurately deploy mobile clinics, volunteer shuttles, or pharmacy home-delivery signups directly where accessibility gaps are highest.

Target SDG: Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-Being)

The Domain Logic: Healthcare access isn't just about insurance; it’s about geography. Low-income or rural areas often lack pharmacies, forcing vulnerable populations to travel hours for life-saving prescriptions.

Public Data Sources: Homeland Infrastructure Foundation-Level Data (HIFLD) for pharmacy locations combined with US Census Bureau demographic data.

High-Level Execution Plan: Build a geospatial dashboard using Leaflet.js or Mapbox. Prompt your AI to calculate driving distances or public transit routes from low-income census tracts to the nearest pharmacy, flagging areas with a high "accessibility gap."

Value & Impact: Provides actionable data for mobile clinics and non-profits to deploy resources where they are needed most.
